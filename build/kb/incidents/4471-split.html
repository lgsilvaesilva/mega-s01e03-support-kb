  • parser. The shared parser reads every date month first. Our tenants write the day first, so day and month swap: 7 April arrives as 4 July. Every tenant, every channel.
  • hint. The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People type what it says; the form then reads the date the tenant’s way, day first. Only the form channel, only tenants on booking-v3. The parser fix does not touch it: the form needs the hint its tenant writes.
  • one-day move. 5 reports where the date is off by one day, not swapped: “Lisbon got Tuesday, Toronto got Monday. Same meeting.” That is a time zone, not an order of fields. The desk saw them (desk-notes.json) and files them under parser by tenant; they stay counted there, as in the desk’s own table, and are counted again here as their own problem.

Why it merged as one

Decision 0001 merges reports on subject alone. All three problems arrive as “wrong date”, so subject matching cannot tell them apart. Intake’s tag cannot either: it is guessed from the reporter’s browser, not from the tenant, and disagrees with the tenant’s cause on many reports in this ticket. Count by what the report describes and where it came from, not by its subject or its tag.

How the counts are made

  • One id, one report. The export pages overlap, so some reports appear twice; each counts once.
  • The cause comes from the tenant (locale and booking form, causes.ts), never from intakeTag.
  • Counts follow the desk’s own view (CauseTable, as exported): each report under the cause its tenant gives it. The one-day moves stay under parser and also make their own row (problems.ts).
